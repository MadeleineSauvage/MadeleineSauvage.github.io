The End of the First Year of my PhD

If the first year has gone this fast, I can only imagine what the next three will be like.
My project has already flipped on it's head a few times, but at least that' kept me on my toes. Right now I'm wrapping up my progression report and literature review, trying to make the constant flood of information at least somewhat cohesive. I've been trying to get used to having so much control over such a long project, it's been pretty daunting. Having to make so many decisions at every point in the day about what to read, what to code, what deserves writing about, what to ignore, how to revise feedback. I'm not too sure if the feeling of panic will every die down entirely, but I'm getting more comfortable with it.

Next steps is to get back to coding (yay!) and start to reproduce some small models. My supervisor has pointed me to the neutral model (https://www.nature.com/articles/s41562-021-01149-x), seems like a good place to start to see how complexity theoristsmake sense of the world. It's used in such a wide range of domains - biology, information science, social science... And then I can move onto the ABM, the core of the project, and make sure it is ready for me to start year two in a good stead.

I leave for a summer school in just under a month, just after my progression meeting, I'm hoping it'll be a productive bit of fun.
