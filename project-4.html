Sovereign AI: What is Going on?

If it's mine it's better? Is that the logic?
For one, could it ever be if we cannot control the entire supply chain from silica to the energy source? But that isn't really my point anyway, even if we could do it, I wouldn't want it.
The rambles around sovereign AI is (in my view) just the next trend in nationalistic tech spending. The pull inward of control and the push outward of influence. I can't say I am my goverments number one fan, but I'm not particularly worried about their involvement; if anything I'd like them to step up.
I am (very) much more concerned about... the private sector! (who saw that coming?). Because apparently now as long as you're British (or your company is based here) then you must be able to serve the public well. Even from my fairly limited years I think I have enough experience to doubt the sincerity of profit.
Why do we want control, and what over? Are they all just vying for control over the masses of data that can be collected from any one Uk citizen? To keep data secure they must keep data, to keep AI making driven decisions with that data they must first be willing to defer to the AI's decisions.
It may be that I'm a little weary and cynical, but sadly I don't want to change. Public ownership over technology is not the same as sovereign AI. One will allow us to refuse development or shape it. The other is a reaction to the fear of being left behind and the desire for control. It's not all nonsensical, many sovereign AI drives are to have better inclusion of local languages and cultures.
